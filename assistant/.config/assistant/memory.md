## 2026-08-30 — Panel scroll/selection internals

BTT ListView: newest message at contentY == -height, oldest at -contentHeight; both collapse to -height when content fits. A Flickable with content smaller than viewport stops delivering wheel events to children — wheel faces must be siblings. An anchored item as a direct child of a Column silently breaks the positioner — anchor inside the field instead. Touchpad wheel = pixelDelta at event rate: apply 1:1, no animation, no notch quantising. Harness: /usr/lib/qt6/bin/qmltestrunner -platform offscreen -input <file>; console.* prints as QDEBUG. Selection: Selection.qml singleton (registered TextEdits, anchor+head) + SelectableArea; unregistered items are skipped in copies by construction.
