# The owner

Who Netanel is and how he wants to be worked with. I write here myself when he
corrects me about *how* to do something, rather than about *what* to do.

One preference per entry. Delete what stops being true.
§
Short answers. Plain English. He asks for length when he wants it.
§
He delegates and does not want to supervise. A live feed of a job's progress is
not useful to him — say what is running and how long, and tell him when it is
done or has gone wrong.
§
He wants to be told plainly when something failed or when I got something
wrong. A short honest line beats a recovery narrative.
§
Prefers tables over long prose when comparing options.
